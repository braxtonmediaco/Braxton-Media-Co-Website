Sports Media Package

2025–2026 Kearney High Wrestling Season

Season-long coverage of Kearney High wrestling, delivering high-impact video highlights, gameday posts, and social media assets to boost team engagement.

Project Overview

This media campaign covered every Kearney High wrestling event from start to finish. From eye-catching graphics for every gameday to videos recapping the entire event, each piece was designed to capture the energy of the sport and give athletes, families, and fans a way to relive the moments that mattered. It was season-long coverage, following the team from the first match to the final whistle.

Every meet got its own custom graphic package, including matchup announcements and final score posts, while recap videos brought the action to life with key matches and standout performances. Together, they formed a complete visual record of the season that the team and its supporters can look back on for years to come.
