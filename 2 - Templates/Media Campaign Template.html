Sports Media Package

Summer Sports Series Highlight Campaign

A multi-week coverage of regional summer sports, delivering high-impact video highlights, player portraits, and social media assets to boost team engagement.

Project Overview

This campaign was designed to capture the raw energy and dedication of local athletes during the summer tournament season. Over the course of three weeks, Braxton Media Co provided end-to-end media coverage.

The goal was to create a cohesive media package that the teams could use to boost their social media presence, recruit new players, and give families lasting memories of the season. The deliverables included two full-length highlight videos, over 150 edited high-resolution photos, and custom graphics.
